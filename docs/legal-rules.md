# Legal Metrology Compliance Statutory Framework

Reference: **Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (Act No. 1 of 2010)** & **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011**

## Mandatory Declarations Mapping Table

| Rule ID | Rule Subject | Legal Clause | Mandatory Statutory Requirement | Non-Compliance Severity & Penalty |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `LMPC-R06-MFG` | Manufacturer / Packer Identity | Rule 6(1)(a) & (b) | Name and complete physical address of manufacturer/packer with valid postal PIN code. | **FAIL** (Fine up to ₹25,000 for 1st offense under Sec 36(1)) |
| `LMPC-R06-QTY` | Net Quantity & Metric Symbols | Rule 6(1)(d), Rules 12 & 13 | Net quantity in standard metric units (`g`, `kg`, `ml`, `l`, `m`, `N`). Plural symbols (`gms`, `ml.`) and qualifying terms (`approx`, `when packed`) strictly prohibited. | **FAIL** (Sec 36(1) & Sec 36(2) Short Delivery) |
| `LMPC-R06-MRP` | Maximum Retail Price (MRP) | Rule 6(1)(e) | Price formatted as `MRP Rs ... / ₹ ... (inclusive of all taxes)`. | **FAIL / WARNING** (Sec 36(1) overcharging prohibition) |
| `LMPC-R06-USP` | Unit Sale Price (USP) | Rule 6(1)(e) Second Proviso | Required for packages with quantity > 1 kg or > 1 L, expressed per g, per ml, per kg, or per item. | **WARNING** (Mandatory declaration under 2022 amendments) |
| `LMPC-R06-DATES` | Date of Manufacture / Packing | Rule 6(1)(c) | Month and year in which commodity is manufactured / pre-packed (e.g. `05/2026` or `May 2026`). | **FAIL** (Sec 36(1)) |
| `LMPC-R06-CC` | Consumer Grievance Care Cell | Rule 6(1)(h) | Name, designation, physical office address, telephone number, and email address of grievance officer. | **FAIL** (Sec 36(1)) |
| `LMPC-R06-COO` | Country of Origin | Rule 6(1)(aa) | Explicit declaration of country where product was manufactured. | **FAIL / WARNING** |
| `LMPC-R06-IMP` | Importer Declaration | Rule 6(1)(a) Third Proviso | Mandatory name and complete Indian address of importer for imported goods. | **FAIL** (Applicable to imported goods) |
| `LMPC-R06-ECOMM` | E-Commerce Marketplace Alignment | Rule 6(10) | E-commerce digital listings must accurately display all mandatory packaging declarations. | **MISMATCH ALERT** (Consumer Protection E-Commerce Rules) |

## Statutory Penalties under Section 36 of Legal Metrology Act, 2009
- **Section 36(1)**: Penalty for manufacture, sale, or distribution of non-standard packaged commodities:
  - First offense: Fine extending up to **₹25,000**.
  - Second offense: Fine extending up to **₹50,000**.
  - Subsequent offenses: Fine up to **₹1,00,000** or imprisonment up to **1 year**, or both.
